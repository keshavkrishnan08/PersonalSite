Built a computer vision tool that turns a photo of a bearing, flange, or gearbox into a priced coating quote, and wrote the company’s standard operating procedures. I built The quoting tool: a CLIP matcher that finds the closest past coating jobs, rust and paint detection from pixel color, sizes from typical dimensions, pricing equations for five part types, and a Flask app. I also wrote five company-wide SOPs over a five-month term. Later the demo ran out of memory on its server, so I moved CLIP to ONNX Runtime with 8-bit weights: memory fell from 1.6 GB to about 360 MB, with every catalog result unchanged. I learned A model is not shipped until it runs on the hardware people actually have; profiling memory taught me more than tuning accuracy.
