Kilra, Founder, CEO
A symptom and wellness tracker for iPhone that shows the real triggers behind how you feel. I built the whole app: people log symptoms, mood, sleep, and medications, and on-device statistics test which of those actually go with their worse days, with Apple Health and weather context and a report to bring to a doctor. It is live on the App Store, rated 4.4, with about $10K in annualized revenue. Swift, SwiftUI, SwiftData, HealthKit, CloudKit, StoreKit 2, on-device statistics App StoreWebsiteGitHub
CropConnect, Founder
Done-for-you local produce sourcing for restaurants. We find and vet the farms and run delivery, so kitchens can put named local farms on the menu and charge more for it. I built the site and the operator app behind it: a margin model that prices a menu with local produce, farm ranking, and supply contracts with quality specs. Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase (Postgres, auth), Leaflet, Vercel cropconnect.devGitHub
