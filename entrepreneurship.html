Kilra, Founder, CEO
A symptom and wellness tracker for iPhone that shows the real triggers behind how you feel. People log symptoms, mood, sleep, and medications, and Kilra works out which of those actually go with their worse days. I built The whole iOS app: 13-step onboarding, four-step symptom logging, a wellness score, on-device trigger statistics (Welch t-tests and Pearson correlations), symptom clustering, a severity forecast, Apple Health and weather context, iCloud sync, PDF and CSV reports to bring to a doctor, and subscriptions. Challenge Making patterns trustworthy from one person’s month of data. Every trigger is tested for significance before it is shown, and all of it runs on the phone, so health data never leaves the user’s own iCloud. I learned A simple statistic people can check beats an impressive model they cannot. I trained a Core ML model, but kept it out of the scoring until it can learn from real data. Results Live on the App Store (version 4.1), rated 4.4 from 8 ratings, with about $10K in annualized revenue. Swift, SwiftUI, SwiftData, HealthKit, CloudKit, StoreKit 2, on-device statistics App StoreWebsiteGitHub
CropConnect, Founder
Done-for-you local produce sourcing for restaurants. We find and vet the farms and run delivery, so a kitchen can put named local farms on the menu and charge more for it. I built The site and the operator app: a margin model that prices a menu with local produce, a menu parser, farm ranking by crop match, reliability, distance, and price, supply contracts with quality specs and service credits, a profit recovery tool, accounts, and the meeting request flow. Challenge Keeping a client-heavy app consistent: a deterministic first render so the server and browser draw the same page, and a debounced writer that never runs two saves at once and always keeps the newest state. I learned The software was the easy half. The business is supply and trust, and a chef is persuaded by a margin calculation on their own menu, not by a feature list. Results Live at cropconnect.dev. Still early: the farm network is a seed list of six Indianapolis-area farms, and payments are not wired in yet. Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase (Postgres, auth), Leaflet, Playwright, Vercel cropconnect.devGitHub
VolunteerU, Cofounder
A free platform where students start volunteer organizations and log hours that their organizers verify. I built Most of the app (76 of 99 commits): the screens, the organization workspace, listings, the local store, and cloud sync. Aryav Das added the photo card redesign, the nonprofit map, the database migrations, and security fixes. Challenge Never losing a volunteer’s hours: a durable sync outbox that retries with backoff and refuses to overwrite cloud data with a stale or empty copy. I learned Sharing a codebase made me write code another person can change safely, with clear states for loading, empty, and failed. Results Live at volunteeruapp.com. Organization verification is still manual, and the launch to real users is next. Next.js, React, Supabase (Postgres, auth), PostHog volunteeruapp.comGitHub
