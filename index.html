## Ventures

### Kilra: Digital Health OS
Founder, CEO. I built and lead the product end to end, from the on-device statistics to the AI-driven growth. People log symptoms, medications, sleep, and stress, and Kilra tests which factors go with their worse days, surfacing the real triggers behind how they feel, so they can adjust their lifestyle quickly instead of waiting on expensive, drawn-out doctor visits.
- $10K annualized revenue
- Tech: Swift, SwiftUI, SwiftData, HealthKit, CloudKit, Core ML, scikit-learn
- GitHub: https://github.com/keshavkrishnan08/Kilra
- iOS, live on the App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kilra-symptom-tracker/id6760767331

### CropConnect
Founder. Done-for-you local produce sourcing for restaurants. Independent kitchens keep three to five cents on every dollar and have already cut everything they can, so the only real lever left is charging more, and food from a named farm down the road is how they do it: the menu price moves, the food cost barely does, and that gap is almost pure profit. CropConnect runs the entire supply side, finding and vetting the farms and delivering every week, so the kitchen just cooks.
- Tech: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase, Leaflet, Vercel
- Live: https://cropconnect.dev

## Selected research

### Safe Fast Charging Robust to Battery Aging
Sole author (accepted at IEEE IECON 2026). A safety filter for lithium-ion fast charging with no optimizer in the loop: a fixed bisection on a reduced-order cell model. In simulation it kept temperature and voltage within limits on all 156 aged-cell draws, at a median 56 microseconds per step, while standard fast-charging protocols broke the limits in all 40 warm conditions tested.
- Tech: Python, NumPy, SciPy, OSQP baseline
- GitHub: https://github.com/keshavkrishnan08/safe-charge

### The Carbon Effect of Flexible Computing Loads: A Distribution-Free Theory of Dispatch, Investment, and Optimal Contracts
1st Author, Project Coordinator, George Mason University, Costello College of Business (ASSIP). Modeled the power draw of AI data centers and crypto mining and showed that it raises grid carbon unless the load runs on surplus renewables, built on distribution-free equilibrium cutoffs and an exact closed form for the change in carbon, each checked numerically. Earlier in the program I stress-tested published bitcoin-mining emission claims against EIA generation data to see which ones held up. Research under Dr. Jiasun Li; presented at the George Mason student conference and published in the Journal of Student-Scientists' Research (2026).
- Paper: https://journals.gmu.edu/jssr/article/view/5665
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.13021/jssr2026.5665
- Code (team repository): https://github.com/keshavkrishnan08/flexible-load-carbon
- Tech: Python, NumPy, SciPy, pandas, statsmodels, matplotlib
- Screening analysis: https://github.com/keshavkrishnan08/bitcoin-mining-emissions

## Projects

### ECHO: finding people with Wi-Fi, no cameras
Built a system that turns the radio signals between ordinary Wi-Fi devices into a live 3D picture of where people are, and marks space no link observes as unknown rather than empty. A seeded channel simulator, a signal pipeline, Bayesian radio tomography, and a Hungarian and Kalman tracker run in the browser; a Python port matches the TypeScript engine bit for bit and a C++20 library reproduces the signal processing.
- Tech: TypeScript, Next.js, React Three Fiber, Web Workers, Python, FastAPI, C++20
- GitHub: https://github.com/keshavkrishnan08/ECHOSPACE

### FIREWATCH: computer vision based probabilistic wildfire tracking
Built a system that fuses camera, satellite, weather and terrain into a continuously self-correcting probabilistic spread forecast, then turns it into human-in-the-loop evacuation and resource decisions. Validated it by replaying five real fires against GOES ground truth.
- Tech: Python, PyTorch, YOLO, RT-DETR, SAM 2, U-Net, OpenCV, Shapely, DuckDB, FastAPI, GOES-18 and HRRR data, React, TypeScript, deck.gl, MapLibre
- Live site: https://keshavkrishnan08.github.io/firewatch/
- GitHub: https://github.com/keshavkrishnan08/firewatch

### CROSSING: computer vision based pedestrian-vehicle safety intelligence
Built a system that watches ordinary street video, models every person, vehicle, and interaction in the scene, and predicts who a vehicle is about to hit. A learned interaction model beats an engineered time-to-collision score at forecasting near-misses (0.95 AUROC), holds its calibration, and flags a pedestrian's intent to cross roughly 2.8 seconds before they step into the road.
- Tech: Python, YOLOv3 (ONNX Runtime), OpenCV, ByteTrack, Kalman filters, homography, NumPy GRU, scikit-learn, FastAPI
- Live site: https://crossing-pi.vercel.app
- GitHub: https://github.com/keshavkrishnan08/ProjectCROSSING

### GridWatch: Indiana's data centers mapped against the power grid
Creator. Built an interactive atlas that plots every proposed and existing data center in Indiana against the grid: megawatts, water use, IURC dockets, and a projected household bill impact, with every figure linked to the public filing it came from. It tracks 76 projects across 32 counties whose active capacity already adds up to roughly 15,100 MW, about 43% of Indiana's peak demand, on a grid that is still 61% coal. A 2020 to 2035 timeline scrubs the build-out and a transparent calculator turns filed costs into a projected bill.
- Tech: TypeScript, Vite, MapLibre GL, D3, Python, Overpass API, Vitest, Playwright
- Live: https://gridwatch-indiana.vercel.app
- GitHub: https://github.com/keshavkrishnan08/GridWatch

### Forecasting crop waste across the U.S. supply chain
Inventor (provisional patent, 26 claims). Built a graph neural network that forecasts supply-chain-driven crop waste across 2,130 US counties by routing inter-regional economic shocks through a cascade diffusion model.
- Tech: PyTorch, scikit-learn, XGBoost, statsmodels, GeoPandas
- GitHub: https://github.com/keshavkrishnan08/CasCrop
