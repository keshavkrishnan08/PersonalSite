Selected projects

FIREWATCH, wildfire tracking
Combines camera, satellite, weather, and terrain data into a forecast of where a wildfire will spread, and turns it into evacuation decisions a person approves. Live siteGitHub
CROSSING, pedestrian safety
Watches ordinary street video and predicts which pedestrians a vehicle is about to hit, before anyone steps into the road. Live siteGitHub
GridWatch, data centers and the power grid
An interactive map of every data center planned or running in Indiana, set against the power grid, with each figure linked to its public filing. Live mapGitHub
CasCrop, crop waste forecasting
A graph neural network that predicts which harvests will go to waste by following price shocks through the U.S. supply chain. Filed as a provisional patent. GitHub
