Selected projects

FIREWATCH, computer vision based probabilistic wildfire tracking
Built a system that fuses camera, satellite, weather and terrain into a continuously self-correcting probabilistic spread forecast, then turns it into human-in-the-loop evacuation and resource decisions. Validated it by replaying five real fires against GOES ground truth. Python, PyTorch, YOLO detector, data assimilation, deck.gl Live siteGitHub
CROSSING, computer vision based pedestrian-vehicle safety intelligence
Built a system that watches ordinary street video, models every person, vehicle, and interaction in the scene, and predicts who a vehicle is about to hit. A learned interaction model beats an engineered time-to-collision score at forecasting near-misses (0.95 AUROC), holds its calibration, and flags a pedestrian’s intent to cross roughly 2.8 seconds before they step into the road. Python, YOLOv3, multi-object tracking, trajectory forecasting, FastAPI Live siteGitHub
GridWatch, Indiana’s data centers mapped against the power grid
Built an interactive atlas that plots every proposed and existing data center in Indiana against the grid: megawatts, water use, IURC dockets, and a projected household bill impact, with every figure linked to the public filing it came from. It tracks 76 projects across 32 counties whose active capacity already adds up to roughly 15,100 MW, about 43% of the state’s peak demand, on a grid that is still 61% coal. A 2020 to 2035 timeline scrubs through the build-out, and a transparent calculator turns filed costs into a projected bill. Live atlasGitHub
Forecasting crop waste across the U.S. supply chain, Inventor
Built a graph neural network that forecasts supply-chain-driven crop waste across 2,130 US counties by routing inter-regional economic shocks through a cascade diffusion model. Filed as a provisional patent with 26 claims. GitHub
