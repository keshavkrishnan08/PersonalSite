Selected projects

ECHO, Wi-Fi sensing
Finds people with Wi-Fi, no cameras. It turns the radio signals between ordinary Wi-Fi devices into a live 3D picture of where people are, and marks space no signal reaches as unknown instead of empty. A channel simulator feeds a signal pipeline, Bayesian radio tomography reconstructs where people are in 3D, and a Hungarian and Kalman tracker follows each one; a Python port matches the TypeScript engine bit for bit. TypeScript, Next.js, React Three Fiber, Web Workers, Python, FastAPI, C++20, Bayesian tomography, Kalman filters, Hungarian algorithm GitHub
FIREWATCH, wildfire tracking
Combines camera, satellite, weather, and terrain data into a forecast of where a wildfire will spread, and turns it into evacuation decisions a person approves. Smoke detection feeds an ensemble of physics-based spread runs, a particle filter reweights them as new observations arrive, and a learned surrogate makes the forecast fast. Python, PyTorch, YOLO, RT-DETR, SAM 2, U-Net, OpenCV, Shapely, DuckDB, FastAPI, GOES-18 and HRRR data, React, TypeScript, deck.gl, MapLibre Live siteGitHub
CROSSING, pedestrian safety
Watches ordinary street video and predicts which pedestrians a vehicle is about to hit, before anyone steps into the road. It detects and tracks every person and car, maps them onto the road in meters, and a recurrent network I wrote from scratch in NumPy estimates who is about to cross. Python, YOLOv3 (ONNX Runtime), OpenCV, ByteTrack, Kalman filters, homography, NumPy GRU, scikit-learn, FastAPI Live siteGitHub
GridWatch, data centers and the power grid
An interactive map of every data center planned or running in Indiana, set against the power grid, with each figure linked to its public filing. A Python pipeline gathers the data from OpenStreetMap and public records, and a static TypeScript map adds a build-out timeline and a household bill calculator. TypeScript, Vite, MapLibre GL, D3, Python, Overpass API, Vitest, Playwright Live mapGitHub
