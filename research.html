Safe Fast Charging Robust to Battery Aging, 1st Author
A lightweight safety layer that keeps lithium-ion fast charging within safe limits as the battery ages. Instead of solving an optimization at every step, it runs a short, fixed bisection on a reduced-order electro-thermal cell model, tested against a full physics simulation of aged cells. Accepted at IEEE IECON 2026. Python, NumPy, PyBaMM (Doyle-Fuller-Newman model), OSQP and PPO-Lagrangian baselines GitHub
The Carbon Effect of Flexible Computing Loads, 1st Author, Project Coordinator
When AI data centers and crypto miners can turn their power use up and down, do they make the grid cleaner or dirtier? A game-theoretic model of dispatch, investment, and contracts that holds for any demand distribution, checked against U.S. grid data from the EIA. The answer: dirtier, unless the load runs on surplus renewable power. Published in the Journal of Student-Scientists’ Research at George Mason University, from ASSIP under Dr. Jiasun Li. Python, NumPy, SciPy, pandas, statsmodels, matplotlib PaperDOIScreening analysis

Crop Waste Forecasting, Inventor
A graph neural network that predicts which harvests will go to waste by following price shocks through the U.S. supply chain. Price drops spread along a commodity graph and price rises along a geographic one, and a small neural network turns both into a forecast for each county. Filed as a U.S. provisional patent. PyTorch, PyTorch Geometric, scikit-learn, XGBoost, statsmodels, GeoPandas, Google Earth Engine GitHub
