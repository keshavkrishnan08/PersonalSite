Safe Fast Charging Robust to Battery Aging, Sole author
A safety filter that keeps lithium-ion fast charging within temperature and voltage limits as the battery ages, with no optimizer in the loop: a fixed bisection on a reduced-order cell model, in 36 lines of NumPy. In simulation it stayed within limits on all 156 aged-cell draws at a median 56 microseconds per step, where standard fast-charging protocols broke them. Accepted at IEEE IECON 2026. Python, NumPy, SciPy, OSQP baseline GitHub
The Carbon Effect of Flexible Computing Loads, 1st Author, Project Coordinator
When do AI data centers and crypto miners, loads that can switch off at will, raise a grid’s carbon emissions? I led the carbon theory and the optimal contract and integrated our four-person team’s work. The answer: a large flexible load raises emissions unless it runs only on renewable power that would otherwise be wasted. Published in the Journal of Student-Scientists’ Research at George Mason University, from ASSIP under Dr. Jiasun Li. Python, NumPy, SciPy, closed-form analysis, numerical verification PaperDOICodeScreening analysis

Crop Waste Forecasting, Inventor
Forecasts which corn, soybean, and wheat harvests will go to waste across 2,130 U.S. counties by following price shocks through the supply chain: price drops travel a commodity graph and price rises a geographic one. It reaches an AUC of 0.935 against 0.917 for local features alone. Filed as a U.S. provisional patent. Python, PyTorch, scikit-learn, XGBoost, statsmodels, GeoPandas GitHub
